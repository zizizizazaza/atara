II. Architecture

A substrate everyone shares.
A parachain you don't.

Every parachain inherits NeoBFT's deterministic finality and Block-STM's parallel EVM. But each one owns its gas market, its governance, and (optionally) its VM.

Adding a parachain does not cost throughput on the others — it adds throughput overall. Cross-parachain transfers settle through an IBC-style atomic relay. No bridge custodian.
